ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

Where India’s Bakery Industry Comes Together

TAMIL NADU BAKERS FEDERATION - INDIA is proud to announce the 7th Edition of the INDIA BAKERY EXPO, scheduled to take place from 18th to 20th September 2026 at the Chennai Trade Centre, New Building Ground Floor, Nandambakkam, Chennai.

This prestigious event will serve as a dynamic and exclusive platform for the bakery industry in South India. Designed as a B2B Trade Fair, the India Bakery Expo is poised to return stronger, larger, and more impactful than ever before. It promises to be a comprehensive hub, offering valuable exposure and opportunities in a rapidly evolving bakery landscape.

THE EXPO AIMS

Driving transformation, knowledge sharing, and commercial excellence across the Indian baking landscape.

Product & Tech Launches

Promote new product launches, innovative technologies, bakery equipment, ingredients, and accessories.

Sourcing & Networking

Act as a sourcing and networking platform for the latest industry trends and market demands.

Sector Linkages

Strengthen linkages between the bakery and food processing sectors.

Technique & Tool Showcase

Showcase cutting-edge developments in bakery techniques, tools, and practices.

Knowledge Dissemination

Disseminate knowledge about best practices in the bakery sector.

Youth & Entrepreneurship

Inspire and encourage the younger generation to explore entrepreneurship and careers in the baking industry.

Standalone & Vital Industry Recognition

Emphasize the importance of the bakery industry as a standalone and vital sector, garnering attention and support from the Government, Media, and Society.

Join us in celebrating the growth, innovation, and potential of South India's vibrant bakery industry at this landmark event.
